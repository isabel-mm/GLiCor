Las estadísticas de categorías y cobertura se calculan sobre los términos; los recursos (corpus, herramientas y estándares) se cuentan aparte. Category and coverage statistics are calculated from the terms; resources (corpora, tools and standards) are counted separately.

There is a gap when a language's subcorpus documents no native designation for the concept: only the loanword appears, or no form appears at all and the glossary proposes one.
